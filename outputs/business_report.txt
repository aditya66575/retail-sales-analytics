SUPERMARKET SALES ANALYSIS — BUSINESS SUMMARY
================================================

Overview
--------
This report summarises 5,053 records dated from 01 Jan 2024 to 31 Dec 2024.
Sales amount is calculated as Unit price × Quantity. Currency is not labelled in the columns reviewed.

Headline metrics
----------------
Total calculated sales: 942,544.85
Total units sold: 23,211
Records analysed: 5,053

Highest sales by category
-------------------------
Product line: Home & Lifestyle (197,260.56)
Branch: Brooklyn (319,705.48)

April–May 2024 comparison
------------------------
April calculated sales: 132,083.88
May calculated sales: 60,955.78
Change in calculated sales: -53.9%
Change in record count: -6.7%
Change in units sold: -51.8%
Change in average units per record: -48.3%

Interpretation
--------------
Calculated sales fell much more sharply than record count between April and May.
The reduction coincided with lower units sold and lower average quantity per record.
Food & Beverages calculated sales increased between those months, while the other
product lines declined in the provided comparison.

Data quality checks
-------------------
Initial inspection found 0 missing values and 0 exact duplicate rows.

Non-positive unit prices: 0
Non-positive quantities: 0

Limitations
-----------
- Sales is calculated from unit price multiplied by quantity.
- Currency is not explicitly labelled in the columns reviewed.
- The dataset has no cost data, so profit cannot be estimated.
- Rows are called records because a unique order ID was not verified.
- This analysis describes patterns; it does not establish causes.
- Verify the dataset's licence and attribution terms before public redistribution.
